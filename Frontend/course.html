OUR COURSES

BBA

Bachelor of Business Administration

A program that focuses on developing leadership, management, and essential business skills. It provides a strong foundation in areas such as finance, marketing, human resources, and operations, while enhancing analytical thinking and decision-making abilities. The program also helps students improve communication teamwork, and problem-solving skills..

BIM

Bachelor of Information Management

It is a combines information technology knowledge with modern management practices. It equips students with skills in IT systems, data management, and business processes while developing leadership, analytical, and decision-making abilities. The program prepares graduates to effectively manage information resources and adapt to the technological needs of organizations..

BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications

It is a program that focuses on programming and software development. It provides students with a strong foundation in computer science, coding, and application development while enhancing problem-solving and analytical skills. The program prepares graduates for careers in software development, data analysis, managing database, IT services, and the technology industry..
